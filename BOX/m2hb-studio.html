Form. Material. Motion.

An M2HB-inspired emplacement, built as an interactive visual study. Everything is live 3D, rendered locally. No CDN, external model, or internet connection is needed.

Inside the viewer

  • Procedural meshes at five selectable detail levels.
  • GGX shading, grain-scale roughness, machined steel, wood, brass, copper, and painted surfaces.
  • A 64-round instanced belt on an arc-length spline, with synchronized feeding, recoil, action and ejection.
  • Brief muzzle flash, layered depth-softened smoke, local heat haze and separate case/link trajectories with floor collisions.
  • Four inspection cameras and clean PNG export up to 8K, subject to graphics memory.

Scope. A decorative browser prototype, not an exact mechanical reconstruction. Production UVs, a skinned source rig, baked 4K albedo/normal/ORM maps and UE5/Unity project files are not included. Smoke and heat are visual approximations, not a fluid simulation. Capture resolution does not add source-mesh or texture detail.

Firing produces brief flashing light. Reduced-motion preferences disable flash by default. Sound is optional and starts muted.
